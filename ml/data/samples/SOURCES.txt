Dataset: SMS Spam Collection v.1
Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository
URL: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
Citation: Almeida, T., Hidalgo, J., Yamakami, A. (2011). SMS Spam Collection [Dataset].
UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CC84

180 rows were sampled (seed 20260801, ml/data/raw/sample_uci.py) from the 5,574-row corpus: 70
of the 747 available "spam" rows and 110 of the 4,827 available "ham" rows, oversampling spam
relative to its natural ~13% share so the resulting toy dataset has a usable scam/genuine mix.

Known limitations of this sample, worth stating plainly (design.md's own targets -- see
ml/EXPERIMENTS.md and HANDOFF.md for the full account):
- English only. No Hindi, Hinglish, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, or Marathi rows.
- UK/Singapore SMS from ~2011-2011, not Indian bank/UPI messages. Nothing in the "genuine hard
  negative" bucket design.md section 9.2 calls "the project" -- every genuine (ham) row here is
  an ordinary personal message, not a real transactional bank alert.
- Scam categories (KYC_PHISHING, LOTTERY_PRIZE, etc.) were assigned by a keyword heuristic
  against the dataset's own ground-truth spam/ham labels, not by a human labeler -- see each
  row's `labeled_by` field and ml/scripts/label_uci_sample.py's own docstring.
- 180 rows total, far short of design.md's >=12,000-row target.

This is an intentionally small, honestly-scoped starting corpus for exercising the pipeline
end to end on real data, not a claim that design.md's Phase 2 targets are met.
